About this policy

This policy explains how ZS Games (“we”, “us”) handles information when you play Market Simulator (“the App”). The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name or email address to play.

Information processed by the App and its service providers

We use Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform (UMP), Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Billing. These services may process the following information:

  • Gameplay and app activity: session and app events, game-day progress, customer counts, in-game balance and day results, store interactions, purchase product identifiers and outcomes, and rewarded-ad placement and completion events.
  • Advertising information: IP address, advertising or app-instance identifiers, ad requests and interactions, and information used for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. The exact data may depend on your device settings, consent choices, and the services’ configuration.
  • Diagnostics: crash traces, app version, operating system, device model, and technical information needed to diagnose stability issues.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes payments. We receive purchase information needed to verify the transaction and provide the item. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Game progress is stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system or a cloud save service for player profiles.

How we use information

We use gameplay and diagnostic information to understand how the App performs, fix crashes and bugs, improve game balance and features, administer in-app purchases, deliver requested ads, measure ad performance, and protect the services against fraud or abuse.

Advertising choices

AdMob’s UMP consent flow is used to present privacy choices when required by applicable rules and Google’s policies. Your choices and device advertising settings can affect which ads are available. You can also reset or limit your device advertising identifier in your Android or iOS settings. Google explains its ad and data practices in its Privacy Policy.

Third-party services and sharing

Information described above is transmitted to Google services that provide analytics, crash reporting, advertising, consent management, and purchase processing. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

Retention and deletion

Local game progress can be removed by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App; device backup settings may affect whether local data is later restored. Analytics and crash information is retained and deleted according to the relevant Google/Firebase service settings and policies. Uninstalling the App stops future collection by the App but does not by itself delete information already held by those providers. For a privacy request, contact us using the address below.

Children

The App does not ask players to submit direct identifiers such as a name or email address. Advertising and analytics services may process device and usage information as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern about information processed through the App, please contact us.

Security and international processing

We and our service providers use safeguards intended to protect information. Google services may process information in countries where Google or its service providers operate.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, object to, or request deletion of personal information. To make a request or ask a question about this policy, email zsgames.studio@outlook.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. We will publish the latest version on this page and update the date above.

Uygulama ve hizmet sağlayıcılarının işlediği bilgiler

Google AdMob ve User Messaging Platform (UMP), Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics ve Google Play Billing kullanıyoruz. Bu hizmetler aşağıdaki bilgileri işleyebilir:

  • Oyun ve uygulama etkinliği: oturum ve uygulama olayları, oyun günü ilerlemesi, müşteri sayıları, oyun içi bakiye ve gün sonuçları, mağaza etkileşimleri, satın alınan ürün kimlikleri ve işlem sonuçları, ödüllü reklamın gösterildiği yer ve tamamlanma durumu.
  • Reklam bilgileri: IP adresi, reklam veya uygulama örneği tanımlayıcıları, reklam istekleri ve etkileşimleri ile reklam sunumu, ölçümü, güvenliği ve dolandırıcılığın önlenmesi için kullanılan bilgiler. İşlenen kesin veriler cihaz ayarlarınıza, izin tercihlerinize ve hizmet yapılandırmasına bağlı olabilir.

Oyun ilerlemesi cihazınızda yerel olarak saklanır. Oyuncu profilleri için hesap sistemi veya bulut kayıt hizmeti işletmiyoruz.

Bilgileri nasıl kullanıyoruz

Oyun ve tanılama bilgilerini Uygulamanın performansını anlamak, çökme ve hataları gidermek, oyun dengesini ve özelliklerini geliştirmek, uygulama içi satın alımları yönetmek, talep edilen reklamları sunmak, reklam performansını ölçmek ve dolandırıcılık ya da kötüye kullanımı önlemek için kullanıyoruz.
